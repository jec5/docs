## Program Types

- **Points**<br />Members earn virtual currency for every dollar spent or action taken. Points can be redeemed for discounts, products, or exclusive experiences.
- **Cashback & Rebates**<br />A percentage of eligible sales is returned as currency to the earner's account — whether that's a consumer or a channel partner, where this is often called a rebate. The credit can be applied to future purchases or accumulated toward a payout.
- **Tiers & Benefits**<br />Categorize members or partners into levels — such as Bronze, Silver and Gold for consumers, or Authorized, Preferred and Elite for channel partners — based on engagement or sales volume. Moving up unlocks increasingly valuable perks, such as better rebate rates for partners, creating a sense of status and progression.
- **Sales Contests**<br />Reward partners for hitting an activity or volume threshold, like a set number of qualifying sales within a defined period. Commonly used as SPIFFs in channel programs.
- **Certifications & Specializations**<br />Recognize partners who complete training or product certification with a badge. Badge holdings can then gate eligibility for premium incentives or higher tiers.

## Campaign Types

- **Welcome**<br />Greet new members or newly onboarded partners with an immediate reward or points boost upon joining, encouraging early interaction and setting the tone for a positive program experience.
- **Birthday**<br />Reward members with a gift, discount or points boost on or a specified number of days before their birthday.
- **Tier Upgrade**<br />Reward members or partners when they move to a higher level, reinforcing the value of their continued loyalty or partnership.
- **Program Anniversary**<br />Acknowledge members' or partners' tenure with an annual milestone reward, supporting retention on both sides of the business.
- **Reactivation**<br />Recover lost revenue and reengage your at-risk segment by incentivizing members or partners who haven't made a purchase or recorded a qualifying sale in a while.
- **Currency Multiplier**<br />Drive short-term sales or promote specific shopping or selling windows by increasing the rate at which members or partners earn points, cashback, or rebates.
- **Sprend Threshold**<br />Expand incremental revenue by granting rewards only when a member or partner hits a specific transaction or sales value.
- **Product Mix**<br />Diversify members' shopping habits, clear aging inventory, or focus partners' sell-in efforts by encouraging purchases from specific categories or product lines.
- **Location**<br />Drive foot traffic to underperforming locations, or sales activity in underperforming partner territories, with incentives targeted by geography.
- **Channel**<br />Steer members toward more cost-effective or higher-engagement choices by tying rewards to a specific sales channel.
- **Payment Method**<br />Reduce transaction fees or strengthen strategic partnerships by rewarding members for using a preferred payment method.
