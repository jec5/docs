---

## Program Types

- **Points**<br />Members earn virtual currency for every dollar spent or action taken. Points can be redeemed for discounts, products, or exclusive experiences.
- **Cashback**<br />A percentage of eligible sales is returned to members' accounts. Members can apply this credit to future purchases.
- **Tiers & Benefits**<br />Categorize members into levels like Bronze, Silver and Gold based on their engagement or spending history. As members move up, they unlock increasingly valuable perks, creating a sense of status and progression.

## Campaign Types

- **Welcome**<br />Greet new members with an immediate reward or points boost upon joining, encouraging early interaction and setting the tone for a positive program experience.
- **Birthday**<br />Reward members with a gift, discount or points boost on or a specified number of days before their birthday.
- **Tier Upgrade**<br />Reward members when they move to a higher loyalty level, reinforcing the value of their continued loyalty.
- **Program Anniversary**<br />Acknowledge your members' loyalty and support retention with an annual milestone reward.
- **Reactivation**<br />Recover lost revenue and reengage your at-risk segment by incentivizing members who haven't made a purchase in a while.
- **Currency Multiplier**<br />Drive short-term sales or promote specific shopping windows by increasing the rate at which members earn points or cashback.
- **Sprend Threshold**<br />Expand incremental revenue by granting rewards only when a member hits a specific transaction value.
- **Product Mix**<br />Diversify members' shopping habits or clear aging inventory by encouraging purchases from specific categories or product lines.
- **Location**<br />Drive foot traffic to underperforming locations or boost activity in lagging regions with incentives targeted by geography.
- **Channel**<br />Steer members toward more cost-effective or higher-engagement choices by tying rewards to a specific sales channel.
- **Payment Method**<br />Reduce transaction fees or strengthen strategic partnerships by rewarding members for using a preferred payment method.
