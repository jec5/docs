<Update label="25 November, 2026" tags={["New releases","Improvements"]} description="v1.9">
  Introducing the Member Portal. Give members one place to see their balance and tier, find ways to earn, redeem rewards and track activity. See the new [Member Experience](/build/member-experience) page for a tour.

  You can now [incentivize profile completion](/build/profile-completion): record when members fill in profile fields, reward them with incentives, and limit how often they can earn with caps.

  MCP, AI Weekly Digest, Badges, Churn Prevention and Gamification are now generally available. The integrations page also now lists popular commerce and marketing platforms and our integration partners.
</Update>

<Update label="6 October, 2026" tags={["New releases","Improvements"]} description="v1.8">
  Referrals are here. Let members bring new members into your program, and reward the new member, the referrer, or both, either when the referred member becomes active or when they make their first purchase. Cap how often a member can be rewarded as a referrer, and track referral volume on your dashboard.
</Update>

<Update label="16 July, 2026" tags={["New releases","AI"]} description="v1.7">
  Autonomous Incentive Management is here. Fielo AI can now continuously monitor incentive performance and apply optimizations automatically or with manual approval, based on the trust level you configure for each incentive.
</Update>

<Update label="11 June, 2026" tags={["New releases","AI"]} description="v1.6">
  Introducing Loyalty Designer. Chat with our AI advisor to rapidly ideate and build a loyalty program strategy tailored to your business goals, cutting time-to-launch by up to 70%.
</Update>

<Update label="7 May, 2026" tags={["New releases","AI"]} description="v1.5">
  Segment Agent is now available. Create and edit segments through a simple chat-based experience, leveraging your member attributes without needing to know the underlying data model.
</Update>

<Update label="2 April, 2026" tags={["New releases","AI"]} description="v1.4">
  Introducing Incentive Agent. Brainstorm, create, and edit incentives and rules by chatting with Fielo AI, no data model knowledge required.
</Update>

<Update label="26 February, 2026" tags={["New releases","AI"]} description="v1.3">
  Program Agent has launched. Chat with Fielo AI on any program page to get performance reviews, compare incentive impact, and explore ideas for improving your program.
</Update>

<Update label="22 January, 2026" tags={["New releases","Improvements"]} description="v1.2">
  Redemption Ceiling Rules are now available. Define how much currency a member can apply to a future purchase or presale, with configurable tiebreakers when multiple rules apply.
</Update>

<Update label="18 December, 2025" tags={["New releases","Improvements"]} description="v1.1">
